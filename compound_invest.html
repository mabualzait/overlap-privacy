Privacy Policy

Application: Compound Growth & Wealth Calc • Effective Date: September 18, 2026

100% Offline & Private by Design

We believe financial planning and wealth projections should remain strictly between you and your screen. Compound Growth & Wealth Calc contains zero external network permissions, zero analytics SDKs, zero trackers, and zero backend databases.

1. Summary & Core Philosophy

This Privacy Policy describes how Compound Growth & Wealth Calc (package com.compound.invest) handles your information. In short: we do not collect, process, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal or financial data.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Initial investment balances
  • Monthly deposit amounts
  • Interest rates & compounding intervals
  • Inflation & annual step-up configurations
  • Saved named scenarios & comparison records

What We Collect

  • No personally identifiable information (PII)
  • No email addresses, names, or accounts
  • No financial balances or banking links
  • No location data or device identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioral usage metrics

2. Android Manifest & Network Architecture

Unlike apps that promise privacy through policy alone, Compound Growth & Wealth Calc enforces privacy at the operating system level:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its manifest. It is physically impossible for the app to open sockets, connect to external servers, or upload telemetry.
  • No Network State: The app does not request or check network connectivity (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).
  • No Telemetry / Crash Tracking: No third-party SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Crashlytics) are bundled in this application.

3. Local Device Storage

All your financial simulations, custom rates, and saved comparison scenarios are stored strictly inside the app's sandboxed private storage on your device:

  • Jetpack DataStore Preferences: Saves basic user display preferences (such as selected currency symbol, default calculation frequencies, and timing choices) locally.
  • Room SQLite Database: Encapsulates named investment scenarios that you explicitly choose to save for future comparison.

This data never leaves your device sandbox. If you uninstall the app or clear app storage via Android Settings, all stored scenarios and preferences are permanently deleted.

4. Document Generation & PDF Export

The app offers the ability to generate a comprehensive PDF amortization schedule of your wealth projections.

  • PDF documents are rendered 100% on-device using Android's native android.graphics.pdf.PdfDocument API.
  • Generated files are saved to your chosen local folder or passed via Android's native system share sheet. No external cloud conversion services are ever contacted.

5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

This application does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, cloud computing providers, or data brokers. All charting is custom-rendered directly using Jetpack Compose Canvas, avoiding telemetry-heavy chart libraries.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because the application does not collect, process, or transmit personal data of any kind, it is fully safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in accordance with future platform updates or statutory requirements. Any future revisions will continue to honor our core tenet: zero network transmission and total user privacy.
